# User Personas - Team Toxiic Esports Website

## Primary User Personas

### 1. The Dedicated Fan - "Alex the Supporter"
**Demographics:**
- Age: 18-28
- Location: Global, primarily US/EU
- Gaming Experience: Active gamer, follows multiple esports
- Tech Savvy: High

**Goals:**
- Stay updated with team news and match schedules
- Follow favorite players and their performance statistics
- Purchase team merchandise and exclusive content
- Engage with team content and community
- Watch live streams and match replays

**Pain Points:**
- Difficulty finding match schedules across different time zones
- Lack of detailed player statistics and performance history
- Limited merchandise availability or confusing checkout process
- Slow loading times during peak traffic (match days)
- Poor mobile experience while on-the-go

**User Journey:**
1. Visits homepage for latest news
2. Checks upcoming match schedule
3. Views player profiles and stats
4. Browses merchandise store
5. Engages with social media content

**Key Features Needed:**
- Real-time match updates and notifications
- Comprehensive player profiles with stats
- Streamlined merchandise shopping
- Mobile-optimized experience
- Social media integration

---

### 2. The Aspiring Player - "Jordan the Recruit"
**Demographics:**
- Age: 16-24
- Location: Global
- Gaming Experience: Semi-professional to professional level
- Tech Savvy: High

**Goals:**
- Learn about team structure and recruitment process
- Understand team achievements and reputation
- Find contact information for tryouts
- Research team culture and values
- View current roster and player backgrounds

**Pain Points:**
- Unclear recruitment process or requirements
- Difficulty finding contact information
- Limited information about team culture
- Lack of transparency about team achievements
- No clear path to express interest

**User Journey:**
1. Researches team achievements and reputation
2. Studies current roster and player profiles
3. Looks for recruitment information
4. Contacts team management
5. Submits application or inquiry

**Key Features Needed:**
- Detailed recruitment section
- Clear contact information
- Team achievements showcase
- Transparent team culture information
- Application/inquiry forms

### 3. The Business Partner - "Morgan the Sponsor"

- Role: Marketing manager, business development

- Review team's professional image and brand alignment

- Unclear sponsorship packages or pricing
- Difficult to find professional contact information
- Limited access to marketing materials
- No clear partnership process outlined

**User Journey:**
1. Evaluates team's professional presentation
2. Reviews audience reach and demographics
3. Examines sponsorship opportunities
4. Downloads marketing materials
5. Contacts business development team

**Key Features Needed:**
- Professional business section
- Detailed audience analytics
- Sponsorship packages information
- Marketing material downloads
- Direct business contact forms

### 4. The Media Professional - "Casey the Journalist"
**Demographics:**
- Age: 24-40
- Location: Global
- Role: Esports journalist, content creator, blogger
- Tech Savvy: High

**Goals:**
- Access team press kits and media resources
- Find high-quality images and videos
- Get team and player quotes for articles
- Contact team for interviews and coverage

**Pain Points:**

2. Downloads high-quality images and videos
3. Contacts team for interviews or quotes

## User Persona Summary

### Common Needs Across All Personas:
- **Fast Loading Times**: All users expect quick page loads
- **Mobile Responsiveness**: Most users access on mobile devices
- **Clear Navigation**: Easy to find desired information
- **Updated Content**: Fresh, relevant information
- **Professional Presentation**: Credible and trustworthy appearance

### Key Insights for Design:
1. **Content Hierarchy**: News and matches should be prominently featured
2. **Contact Accessibility**: Multiple contact methods for different purposes
3. **Performance Priority**: Fast loading is crucial for all user types
4. **Mobile-First**: Design should prioritize mobile experience
5. **Professional Balance**: Appeal to both casual fans and business partners

### Design Implications:
- Homepage should feature rotating hero content (matches, news, achievements)
- Clear navigation with dedicated sections for different user types
- Prominent call-to-action buttons for key actions
- Professional design that appeals to sponsors while exciting fans
- Comprehensive search functionality for quick information access
