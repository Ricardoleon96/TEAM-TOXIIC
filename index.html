About Team Toxiic

Our story, mission, and values

More Than a Team. A State of Mind.

Team Toxiic represents the pinnacle of competitive gaming excellence. Founded on the principles of dedication, innovation, and relentless pursuit of victory, we've established ourselves as a formidable force in the esports landscape.

Our organization brings together the most talented players, experienced coaches, and dedicated support staff to create an environment where champions are forged. We don't just play games – we dominate them.

The "Toxiic" mentality isn't about being negative; it's about being so skilled, so strategic, and so dominant that we become the poison our opponents can't cure. Every match is an opportunity to prove that excellence isn't just a goal – it's our standard.
